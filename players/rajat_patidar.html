Methodology

Evaluated 883 IPL & T20I matches (2022–2026) to isolate 178 high-pressure games (MLI ≥ 30: knockouts, Super Overs, or close finishes). Players are ranked via the calibrated CPPI combining Bayesian HP Win Rate (35%), Normalized Pressure Lift Δ (25%), Knockout Win Rate (20%), HP POTM Density (10%), and Baseline Win Rate (10%), scaled by a sigmoid credibility curve and BCCI auction eligibility filters.

Thought Process

Traditional averages reward stat-padding in one-sided matches. Measuring True Pressure Lift (Δ) isolates who genuinely raises their team’s win rate when stakes peak — highlighting high-value clutch winners like Shivam Dube and Jacob Duffy suited for Sawai Mansingh Stadium (Jaipur) within a ₹45 Cr auction budget, rather than overpaying for reputation.
