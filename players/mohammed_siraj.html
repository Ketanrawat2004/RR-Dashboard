1. Key Capability & Technical Execution
Aggressive new-ball strike bowler who excels when hitting the seam upright in overs 1-6. Generates sharp movement through the air and off the pitch, with a 60.0% powerplay dot-ball percentage when conditions offer early moisture.
2. Comparative Performance vs Peer Group
Exhibits extreme performance variance under hyper-pressure conditions. While effective with early swing, Siraj's economy inflates to 10.4+ RPO in death overs (overs 17-20) when required run rates exceed 10.0 RPO. In playoff knockouts and close finishes (≤ 15 runs), his win differential drops by -5.8% compared to disciplined control pacers like Josh Hazlewood and Jacob Duffy (+20.5% Lift).
3. Tactical Deployment: Sawai Mansingh Stadium, Jaipur
Sawai Mansingh Stadium offers swing for only the first 2-3 overs under lights. Once the ball softens and night dew sets in by over 14, Siraj's preferred back-of-a-length deliveries sit up into the hitting zone of modern power-hitters, exposing RR's death-bowling defense.

Methodology

Evaluated 883 IPL & T20I matches (2022–2026) to isolate 178 high-pressure games (MLI ≥ 30: knockouts, Super Overs, or close finishes). Players are ranked via the calibrated CPPI combining Bayesian HP Win Rate (35%), Normalized Pressure Lift Δ (25%), Knockout Win Rate (20%), HP POTM Density (10%), and Baseline Win Rate (10%), scaled by a sigmoid credibility curve and BCCI auction eligibility filters.

Thought Process

Traditional averages reward stat-padding in one-sided matches. Measuring True Pressure Lift (Δ) isolates who genuinely raises their team’s win rate when stakes peak — highlighting high-value clutch winners like Shivam Dube and Jacob Duffy suited for Sawai Mansingh Stadium (Jaipur) within a ₹45 Cr auction budget, rather than overpaying for reputation.
